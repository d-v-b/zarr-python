Tidied the validation added with the recent boundary audit so each rule has one owner. Index-array selectors are checked against the domain in the caller's dtype and narrowed to `intp` by that check, so a coordinate past the `intp` range is a `BoundsCheckError` naming the value and the domain rather than an `OverflowError`; wire `index_array` leaves are held to the message layer's one definition of an integer, and the rejection names the offending leaf; `DimensionGridLike` again declares chunk ids as `[0, n)` with lookups outside the extent raising, and the planner's negative-chunk-id fallback is gone; the "index array varies over an axis a slice map also reads" rejection is one predicate, the new `IndexTransform.index_array_shares_affine_axis` property. The pointwise chunk-plan oracle draws fewer degenerate domains and reports its reach, and `just lint` pins the same ruff as the root pre-commit configuration.
