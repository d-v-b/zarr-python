`index_array_bounds` is now retained and checked at use instead of rejected at load. `ArrayMap` carries the bounds as an `IndexRange` (TensorStore's `index_range`), `IndexTransform.from_json` and `output_index_map_from_json` lower a document whose `index_array` holds a value outside finite bounds without complaint, and the value fails with `BoundsCheckError` — naming the value, the output dimension and the bounds — when something reads it to address storage: `apply` on a point that gathers it, `intersect`, chunk planning, reading or writing through the transform, composing another index array through it, or collapsing a one-element map to a constant. A selection that avoids the value never meets it, so slicing the offending row away leaves a usable transform, and the range travels unchanged through slicing, translation and affine composition and is emitted by `to_json`, finite when it was given finite. This copies TensorStore, which parses the bounds without consulting the array and raises `OUT_OF_RANGE` at evaluation; the eager `NdselError("invalid_json")` on load is gone. A sentinel on the wrong side (`["+inf", "+inf"]`) is still rejected at load, now regardless of the array's contents, as TensorStore rejects it.
