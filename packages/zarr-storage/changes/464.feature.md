Add `Context`, the resources a `zarr-storage` store's operations draw on: a `ThreadPool` and a `Limiter` per backend resource key, with process-wide defaults. Every store view carries a context (`Store(backend, context=...)`, `with_context`), stores sharing a context share its limits, and `store.limited(n)` bounds a burst of operations within the context's limit. `AsyncStore` now runs a sync-only backend on the context's thread pool, and batches run concurrently under the context.
